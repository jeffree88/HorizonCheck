# Reference fallback coverage

v6.15.0 adds a HorizonXI Wiki lookup fallback for native quest identities that do not yet have structured metadata. These records are intentionally reference-only: they do not invent NPCs, requirements, objectives, items, or rewards. Structured HorizonXI/period-correct historical metadata continues to override these fallback fields as it is added.


v6.17.0 replaces nine San d'Oria reference fallbacks with structured HorizonXI metadata, including NPCs, locations, rewards, requirements, objectives, and required items where available.
